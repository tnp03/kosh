# Governance, Safety & Policy

The research, regulation, and practices aimed at making AI safe, aligned, and beneficial.

## Key Players

- **Anthropic** — Constitutional AI, safety research
- **OpenAI** — Safety team, red-teaming
- **Google DeepMind** — Safety research
- **MIRI** — Machine Intelligence Research Institute
- **Partnership on AI** — Industry coalition
- **EU AI Act** — Regulatory framework
- **NIST AI RMF** — Risk management framework

## Why It Matters

As AI becomes more capable, governance and safety determine whether it remains beneficial. This layer cuts across the entire stack.
