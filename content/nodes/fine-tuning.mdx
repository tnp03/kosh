# Fine-tuning & Alignment

Post-training techniques that adapt foundation models to specific tasks, styles, and safety requirements.

## Key Players

- **OpenAI** — RLHF, instruction tuning
- **Anthropic** — Constitutional AI
- **Hugging Face** — TRL, PEFT, Axolotl
- **Unsloth** — Fast fine-tuning
- **Weights & Biases** — Training monitoring
- **LLaMA-Factory** — All-in-one fine-tuning

## Why It Matters

Fine-tuning is how raw foundation models become useful for specific domains. Alignment techniques determine whether models are safe and helpful.
