# Infrastructure

The cloud platforms, GPU clusters, and data centers where AI models are trained and served.

## Key Players

- **AWS** — SageMaker, Bedrock, EC2 P5 instances
- **Google Cloud** — Vertex AI, TPU clusters
- **Azure** — OpenAI partnership, AI infrastructure
- **CoreWeave** — GPU cloud for AI workloads
- **Lambda Labs** — GPU cloud
- **Together AI** — Inference infrastructure

## Why It Matters

Infrastructure is the bridge between silicon and software. It determines who can access compute, at what cost, and with what reliability.
