# Silicon & Chips

The physical hardware that makes AI compute possible — from NVIDIA's dominant GPUs to custom TPUs and emerging AI chip startups.

## Key Players

- **NVIDIA** — H100, A100, Blackwell architecture
- **AMD** — MI300X, ROCm ecosystem
- **Google** — TPU v4/v5e
- **Groq** — LPU inference chips
- **Cerebras** — Wafer-scale chips
- **Intel** — Gaudi accelerators

## Why It Matters

Silicon is the bottleneck of the AI ecosystem. Training runs and inference costs are directly shaped by available compute. Chip availability determines which organizations can build the largest models.
