# Foundation Models

The large language models and multimodal models that form the base layer for AI applications.

## Key Players

- **OpenAI** — GPT-4, GPT-4o, o1, o3
- **Anthropic** — Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 4
- **Meta** — Llama 3, Llama 4
- **Google** — Gemini 2.0, Gemma
- **Mistral** — Mistral Large, Mixtral
- **DeepSeek** — DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1
- **Alibaba** — Qwen 2.5
- **xAI** — Grok

## Why It Matters

Foundation models are the platform on which AI applications are built. Their capabilities, cost, and openness determine what's possible downstream.
